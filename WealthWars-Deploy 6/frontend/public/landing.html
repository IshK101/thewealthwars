QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

What is the purpose of Wealth Wars?

To practice long-term investing judgment under changing markets and social pressure—not simply memorize financial vocabulary.

Do I need investing experience?

No. The in-game Guide explains every major button, the turn flow and a searchable glossary of finance terms.

Can two or three people play?

Yes. Wealth Wars supports two, three or four players in the same live boardroom.

Is this financial advice?

No. Wealth Wars is an educational strategy game. Its market outcomes are fictional and should not be used to make real investment decisions.

What about inside-information cards?

They are fictional game mechanics. Trading on material non-public information is illegal in real life.

How long is a game?

A complete match lasts 20 rounds, with faster cash flow and level progression designed to keep play intense.
